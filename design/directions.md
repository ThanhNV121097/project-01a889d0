# Bốn Chân — the directions shown to the owner

Three readings of the same clinic. The owner chose the third, **The day on
one screen**, and the site on this branch is built in it.

## The reading

Bốn Chân is two vets and a nurse in one room on Trần Duy Hưng. They see dogs
and cats and turn away everything else rather than pretend. Their customers
are pet owners in the surrounding streets, and they arrive in one of two
states: worried about an animal that is unwell, or doing a routine thing —
a shot, a nail trim, a puppy's first check — that they would rather arrange
without a telephone call. The clinic sells on two things: being plain about
what a visit costs before you come, and Dr Linh being the person a frightened
cat does best with. What it is not is a hospital, a pet shop or a grooming
salon with a vet attached: it takes no payment online and sells nothing.

The owner said the part that matters most is the clinic's own day — a desk
that can see today's list without paper. So a direction here is judged on
whether it makes a dense table of facts look deliberate, not on whether it
makes an animal look cute.

---

## Direction 1 — The price is on the door

- **Suits you if:** your owners want to know what a visit costs and how long
  it takes before they pick a day, and you would rather win them with a plain
  price list in the window than with a mood.

**Colours** `#F7F3EC` ground · `#FFFFFF` surface · `#171310` ink · `#5B544C`
ink-soft · `#0B5D3B` accent · `#FFFFFF` accent-ink

**Faces** display Fraunces (variable, OFL) · body Inter. The house style's one
allowance taken: a display face that is not a sans, because this is a
two-person neighbourhood practice and the price list is the headline. A serif
reads as a notice in the window rather than as a product launch.

**Scale** h1 54px / 1.02, tracking −0.025em · h2 34px · prices 21px tabular ·
labels 11px, 0.14em letterspacing. Low, because the first screen has to carry
all seven services with their durations and prices.

**First screen** Left two thirds: the label, a seven-word headline and the
whole price ledger — service, duration, price, a hairline between each. Right
third: one documentary photograph bleeding top to bottom off the right edge.
No hero photograph behind the words at all.

## Direction 2 — A quiet room for a frightened cat

- **Suits you if:** most of your owners come in worried — the cat that hates
  the carrier, the dog going under for a dental — and what you are really
  selling is Dr Linh's hands and a calm room, not a price.

**Colours** `#14110F` ground · `#1D1916` surface · `#F2EBE3` ink · `#A3988C`
ink-soft · `#E8A33D` accent · `#14110F` accent-ink. A near-black ground with
one warm lamp-coloured mark: nothing like the other two.

**Faces** display Bricolage Grotesque (variable, OFL) · body Inter. A grotesk
with real character rather than a neutral sans, so the headline carries a
voice at 68px on a dark ground.

**Scale** h1 68px / 1.02, tracking −0.03em · h2 40px · service lines 17px with
21px tabular prices · labels 11px, 0.16em. The loosest of the three.

**First screen** Two dark studio animal portraits bleeding off the left and
right edges — a wide one and a narrow strip — with the headline, the two vets'
names and three services with their times and prices in the lit column
between them. Prices are present but secondary.

---

## Direction 3 — The day on one screen

- **Suits you if:** what you actually want from a website is fewer telephone
  calls — owners who book the right twenty-minute slot themselves, and a desk
  that can see the whole day without paper.

**Colours** `#EDF0F2` ground · `#FFFFFF` surface · `#0B1016` ink · `#54616E`
ink-soft · `#1F49E0` accent · `#FFFFFF` accent-ink. A cool clinical ground
with one blue that only ever marks an action or a selection.

**Faces** display Space Grotesk (OFL) · body Inter with tabular figures. The
house style's clean sans, chosen for its figures: this site is mostly numbers.

**Scale** h1 40px / 1.05, tracking −0.02em · h2 24px · table rows 16px with
18px tabular prices · labels 11px, 0.12em. Deliberately the densest of the
three — the screen's job is to hold seven services, two vets and their hours
above the fold.

**First screen** A full-bleed band of two clinic photographs across the top,
with the booking panel — step one, the services with duration and price; step
two, the two vets and the days they work — overlapping 64px up into it.

---

## Notes that hold for all three

- The first screen carries the seven services with their stated durations and
  prices, the two vets and what each is for, the opening hours and the street.
- No telephone number, no address number, no reviews, no claims — none were
  given, and none was invented.
- The photographs are stand-ins. The clinic has none of its own yet; whichever
  direction wins, those are the shots to replace.
